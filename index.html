Government Guidelines & Criteria

Jagananna Vidya Deevena Guidelines

Government Order (G.O.Ms.No. 115) Higher Education Department

  • Tuition fee reimbursement is provided directly to the bank accounts of mother / natural guardian of the student in four quarterly installments.
  • Valid Income Certificate issued through Prajaseva / Meeseva portal is mandatory.
  • A minimum biometric or facial attendance of 75% across the semester is strictly enforced for fee sanction.
  • Applicable to ITI, Polytechnic, Degree, Engineering, Medicine, Pharmacy, MBA, and MCA courses.
  • Family landholding limits: Below 10 Acres Wetland or 25 Acres Dryland.
